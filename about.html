About Sneake® Away

Sneake Away is a price comparison site for sneakers, not a shop. We do not hold stock and we do not take your payment.

You pick a model and colour. We show offers from retailers that currently list that pair. You complete the purchase on the retailer site. They ship, invoice and handle returns.

We may earn a commission if you buy through a link. Your price does not change.

The catalogue is built from retailer product feeds (Sizeer, Queens, Oxygen). A listed price can still change at checkout; always confirm size, colour, delivery and returns on the shop site.

Estimated delivery, VAT and duty figures on a product page are indicative. The shop’s checkout is the source of truth.
