FAQ

Do you sell the sneakers?

No. The shop in the offer does. You pay and receive from that retailer.

Are pairs authentic?

Authenticity, warranty and returns follow the retailer’s policy. We do not inspect pairs.

Why can a cheap label price be a bad deal?

Without shipping, VAT or duty to your country the shop price is not what you pay. Choose a destination. Use “Calculate price to you” for an indicative total. The shop confirms the final amount.

Why did a shop disappear when I picked a country?

We only keep shops we believe ship to that country. Sizeer.lt is Lithuania; Queens.fr can cover much of the EU (shipping often “info in the shop”). Unknown shipping stays with a note, we do not invent a rate.

Price alerts?

Today they are stored in your browser. Email / push alerts come later.

Cookies?

See Cookies. We use localStorage for language, theme, country and wishlist.
